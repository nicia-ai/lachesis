# Reviewer limitations and nonclaims

The reviewer signature proves control of the corresponding reviewer private key
at signing time only. It does not prove human identity, natural-person
separation, ongoing custody, key destruction or absence of backups.

Distinct author and reviewer public keys and commitments establish distinct
cryptographic principals. Distinct cryptographic principals do not by
themselves prove distinct natural persons.

The review receipt does not contain, select or authorize any submission
credential, OSF operation, external effect or operational authority. Review
passage grants neither submission authority nor study-operation authority.
Separate exact authorization remains required for any later submission.

No OSF or network access, author-private-key access, credential access,
provider/model call, study role, analysis, publication, push, tag, embargo
change or M10 work occurred. M9b remains NO-GO.
