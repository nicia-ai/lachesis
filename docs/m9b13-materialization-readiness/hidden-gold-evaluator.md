# Hidden gold and evaluator preparation

Future gold is an opaque, domain-separated identity over the exact task,
snapshot, typed answer or insufficient-evidence contract, supporting facts, and
chunk/span citation witnesses. It is constructed in a separate secret-bearing
role and independently validated. A constructor reference is never evaluator
truth merely because the constructor supplied it.

The deterministic local evaluator is arm-blind and makes zero model or provider
calls. Its implementation artifact, exact output schema, and validation record
must be content-addressed before any effect. The complete evaluator input and
artifact identity are present before dispatch and retained in a complete
receipt; no failed or missing attempt may receive post-hoc artifact attribution.

Authors cannot see gold, expected citations, labels, opposite-arm outputs,
population-wide structure, ordering, or future identities. This package creates
no gold or evaluator artifact.
