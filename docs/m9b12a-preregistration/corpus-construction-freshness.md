# Corpus construction, freshness, and retired-material quarantine

Construction protocol: `lachesis-m9b12-future-isolated-corpus-construction/1`.

Retired-material protocol: `lachesis-m9b12a-retired-corpus-quarantine@1.0.0`.

Construction remains prohibited until independently verified external
preregistration and separate authorization. Future documents must be newly
created or selected external natural text with exact source and temporal
provenance, immutable content-addressed snapshots, hidden answer/supporting-fact
gold, typed answerable and insufficient-evidence cases, distributed evidence,
prompt-injection/hostile documents, evidence-adaptive opportunities, and a
credible static baseline. TypeGraph is not required and no graph-only advantage
is assumed.

No development fixture may be copied, translated, renamed, paraphrased, or
structurally cloned. Counts-only freshness and leakage audits are independent.

## Retired material

Retired material is any corpus, case inventory, packet set, topology, hidden
reference set, constructor output, materialization, or derived study artifact
that was used by an earlier attempt; exposed outside its authorized role;
contaminated or potentially contaminated; quarantined; superseded; partially
constructed under a failed attempt; accessed by an excluded identity; or derived
from any such material.

Forbidden access includes mounting; opening or reading; listing or enumerating;
searching; hashing; comparing; copying; inspecting metadata or contents;
namespace traversal; importing; reconstructing; deriving new fixtures,
templates, entities, answers, documents, schedules, packets, or seeds; receiving
snippets, filenames, identifiers, ordering, non-public counts, or structural
summaries; and freshness comparison.

A trusted boundary may provide only minimum opaque commitments needed to enforce
non-reuse. The only allowed counts are the public frozen counts: eight families,
three scales, 24 cells, 24 probe pairs, 168 primary pairs, and 192 total pairs.
Commitments cannot reveal identities, ordering, content, or structural mappings.

## Isolation

Before constructor, materializer, operator, evaluator, or analyst startup,
retired namespaces must be unmounted, unaddressable, non-enumerable, absent from
search roots, and unavailable through repository history, caches, temporary
directories, archives, logs, shell history, environment variables, or parent
workspaces. A role-local denylist is insufficient while a namespace remains
accessible. The bootstrap boundary cannot reveal retired paths or identities.

## Fail-closed response

Suspected or confirmed access triggers, before provider effects:

1. Immediate stop.
2. Zero provider/model dispatch.
3. Zero new credential access.
4. Quarantine of the affected role/session.
5. Quarantine of every produced and derived artifact and identity.
6. Permanent prohibition on output reuse.
7. Exclusion of the observer from later influential study roles.
8. Append-only incident and access-provenance records.
9. Independent noninterference assessment.
10. Fresh identities, environment, seed, and corpus construction for restart.
11. No manual cleanup, selective deletion, renaming, or harmless-exposure
    override.

If observer identity or the complete exposure inventory is unavailable, or
noninterference remains unknown, reuse is forbidden.

An inventory is contaminated when any ancestor input, seed, template, source
selection, task-family instantiation, document, entity, answer, supporting fact,
schedule, packet, or randomization decision came from or was influenced by
retired material. Any disclosure or split/lineage contamination remains governed
by the same quarantine. This milestone accesses and constructs no corpus,
retired material, case, gold value, snapshot, seed, packet, or inventory
identity.
