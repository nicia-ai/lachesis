# Disclosure and role-isolation policy

The preregistration may disclose protocol identities, aggregate public counts,
construction rules, analysis/gates, and financial/security controls. It must not
disclose future or retired case/document identities, paths, seeds, hidden gold,
expected citations, credentials, project details, ordering, snippets, metadata,
or structural mappings.

Constructor, materializer, study operator, evaluator, analyst, auditor,
credential custodian, and control-plane bootstrap are distinct roles.
Constructor, materializer, operator, evaluator, analyst, and credential
custodian have no retired-material awareness. Auditor and bootstrap may know
only minimum opaque commitments and cannot access retired namespaces or receive
paths or identities.

No role that observes retired content or identifying structure may later
participate in construction, execution, evaluation, adjudication, analysis, or
campaign control. Study authors and controllers receive no hidden gold. The
operator sees only the public task and permitted execution artifacts. The
evaluator receives only its exact arm-blind post-execution input. Role/session
objects cannot be reused across boundaries.

Any disclosure or suspected retired-material access is recorded append-only,
quarantines the role/session and every derived identity, and prevents provider
effects. Missing observer identity, incomplete exposure inventory, or unknown
noninterference forbids reuse. Outcome-aware replacement and manual sanitation
are prohibited.
