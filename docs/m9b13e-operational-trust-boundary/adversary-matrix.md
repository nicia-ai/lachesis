# M9b.13e adversary matrix

External bytes remain hostile. This includes registry and network responses,
mutable tags, substituted descriptors, OCI objects, tar streams, SPDX,
provenance, and signed-source inputs. Accidental operator error, stale or
contaminated material, model-generated content, and ordinary process
interference also remain within the operational review boundary.

Those adversaries can affect freshness, contamination, arm isolation,
accounting, or replay and are therefore relevant to scientific validity.
Cryptographic binding, strict bounded parsing, immutable identities, private
roots, quarantine, and independent verification remain mandatory.

A malicious process already running as the acquisition UID is outside the
amended boundary. So are malicious root, administrator, kernel, hypervisor, and
physical compromise. Those are host-security adversaries, not adversaries in a
retrieval-quality study. This exclusion is not mitigated by pathname hashing; it
is stated as an explicit assumption and nonclaim.

The study does not establish security against compromise of the trusted local
host, acquisition account, kernel, administrator, or hypervisor.
