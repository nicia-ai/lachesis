# Corpus construction and freshness protocol

Protocol: `lachesis-m9b12-future-isolated-corpus-construction/1`.

Construction may begin only after independently verified external
preregistration. Documents must be newly created or selected external natural
text with exact source and temporal provenance, immutable content-addressed
snapshots, hidden answer/supporting-fact gold, typed answerable and
insufficient-evidence cases, distributed evidence, prompt-injection/hostile
documents, evidence-adaptive opportunities, and a credible static baseline.
TypeGraph is not required and no graph-only advantage is assumed.

No development fixture may be copied, translated, renamed, paraphrased, or
structurally cloned. Counts-only freshness and leakage audits are independent.
Constructor, materializer, operator, and evaluator roles are separate. Study
authors and controllers cannot access hidden gold; operators cannot access it
during execution.

Any disclosure quarantines the case and all derived identities. Any identity,
lineage, or split contamination rejects the affected inventory before provider
access. There is no post-outcome replacement. This milestone constructs no
document, case, gold value, snapshot, seed, or inventory identity.
