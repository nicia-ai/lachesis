# Proposed future authorization texts

None of these authorizations is issued by M9b.12a. The text, prerequisites, and
scope below reproduce the canonical machine-readable package exactly.

## External preregistration

Text: Authorize submission of the exact checksum-bound M9b.12a preregistration
package to the named external registry and no other action.

Prerequisites: Independent review accepts the corrected package digest,
supersession lineage, and exact artifact checksum manifest.

Grants only: One append-only external registration submission; no corpus,
project, credential, count, inference, or campaign action.

## Corpus materialization

Text: Authorize isolated construction and materialization under the registered
corpus and retired-material protocols only.

Prerequisites: External registration is independently verified, retired
namespaces are excluded, and role isolation is active.

Grants only: Fresh corpus, gold, and split-audit production; no provider,
credential, count, inference, probe, or primary execution.

500 USD cap, and project-scoped credential custody.

Prerequisites: Corpus, retired-material, evaluator, schedule, environment,
security, and disclosure reviews pass with immutable identities.

Grants only: Cap and credential activation plus preflight; no count or inference
dispatch.

probe.

Prerequisites: Cap, credential, zero-spend, accounting, persistence,
retired-material, and response-shape preflights pass.

Grants only: At most one count request per frozen class, zero inference, zero
retries, concurrency one.

and schedule.

Prerequisites: Count-only reconciliation and retired-material noninterference

change.

the registered probe gate passed.

Prerequisites: Verified complete probe measurements pass and all safety,
retired-material, accounting, pricing, replay, authorization, and capacity gates

Grants only: The complete immutable 168-pair primary study; no subset,
replacement, retry, or protocol amendment.

## Final closure and credential revocation

Text: Authorize final replay, analysis, accounting and security closure,
credential revocation, and independent absence verification.

Prerequisites: All authorized study effects and retired-material incidents are
terminally accounted and no further provider dispatch is permitted.

Grants only: Closure and revocation actions; no new count, inference, corpus,
analysis-population, publication, or quarantine-override authority.

Every future authorization remains a separate append-only record. Nothing in
this document activates one or permits retired-material quarantine to be
overridden.
