No external registry is selected, accessed, or configured by M9b.13f.

Any later submission requires a new immutable external identity; an embargo at
least as restrictive as registration `63sh5` through `2030-08-01T00:00:00Z`; no
public view-only link; exact immutable attachment hashes; a server submission
timestamp; separate approval where required; independent post-upload byte
verification; and an append-only local receipt.

It is prohibited to modify registration `63sh5`, replace or withdraw it, change
its embargo, publish it, clone something and present it as the original, or
expose protected context publicly.
