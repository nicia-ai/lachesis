# Role topology and irreversible separation

Fifteen roles are bound in `role-topology.json`. Each has one opaque controller
principal, one role, an explicit input-root set, one output namespace, a network
state, source/gold visibility, and only the minimum predecessor knowledge needed
for its task. Program-author and answer roles are untrusted model roles. Corpus
content and ordinary study roles remain outside the trusted computing base.

The source-acquirer is the only prospective role with temporary allowlisted
egress. Every construction, materialization, audit, study, evaluation, analysis,
receipt, and verification role is network-none. Inputs are read-only mounts and
outputs are confined to the named root. No role receives the parent worktree,
history, caches, bootstrap inputs, credentials, shell history, or unrelated
packet structure.

Hidden-gold observation permanently excludes incompatible construction,
operation, authoring, answering, and analysis roles. Cross-packet structure,
retired material, or disallowed topology observation also creates irreversible
exclusions. Incomplete identity, exposure, lineage, isolation, or
noninterference proof quarantines the principal and descendants across all
sessions and processes. There is no cleanup, reset, reassignment, or
rehabilitation.

No operational principal or launch allocation is issued here. Tests use only
nonserializable synthetic canary tokens outside the future identity domain.
