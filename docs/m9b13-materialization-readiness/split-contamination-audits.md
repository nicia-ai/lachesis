# Split, leakage, and contamination audits

Every audit derives its inventory from authoritative materializer output. A
caller cannot provide, select, omit, or reorder cases or observer history.
Development↔probe, development↔primary, and probe↔primary are audited across
family/task/source/document/entity/answer/fact/template/seed/schedule/packet,
evaluator-input, and analysis-input identities.

Semantic audits cover exact and normalized source duplicates, near-duplicate
fingerprints plus independent review, cross-scale semantic-problem leakage,
cross-arm caches or packets, hidden-label disclosure, template/surface clones,
and answer/fact paraphrases. Environment audits cover retired access, prior
contamination ancestry, parent/public worktree and history, archives, backups,
caches, temp, logs, shell history, environment, bootstrap inputs, and the full
operator/principal exposure history.

Unknown, omitted, caller-selected, or incomplete identity, exposure, lineage,
isolation, provenance, or noninterference evidence quarantines the principal,
object, and all descendants and permanently forbids reuse.
