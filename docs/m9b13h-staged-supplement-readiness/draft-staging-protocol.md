# Stage A — private draft staging

Stage A is disabled. A later, separately reviewed exact authority may permit one
from-scratch private OSF Open-Ended Registration draft and only the three bound
initial attachments: the archive, manifest, and external checksum file.

The stage exists to discover the real external representation before any
submission authority is considered. It must enumerate, redownload, and hash
every visible or generated artifact, including license, metadata, form-export,
and renamed objects. It must also confirm private visibility, no view-only link,
and an unchanged registration `63sh5`.

Stage A never permits submission. Failure leaves the exact draft quarantined and
recorded. It may not be silently deleted or recreated; cleanup requires a new
exact authority.
