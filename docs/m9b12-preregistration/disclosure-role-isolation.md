# Disclosure and role-isolation policy

The preregistration may disclose protocol identities, aggregate population
counts, construction rules, analysis/gates, and financial/security controls. It
must not disclose future case/document identities, construction seeds, hidden
gold, expected citations, credentials, or provider project details.

Constructor, materializer, operator, and evaluator roles are independent. The
study author and controller receive no hidden gold. The operator sees only the
public task and permitted execution artifacts. The evaluator receives its exact
arm-blind input after execution. Role/session objects cannot be reused across
boundaries.

Any disclosure is recorded append-only, quarantines affected identities, and
prevents provider effects until an independent audit accepts the complete
remaining frozen inventory. Outcome-aware replacement is prohibited.
