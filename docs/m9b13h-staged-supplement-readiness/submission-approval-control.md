# Stage B — submission and terminal control

Stage B is unauthorized. Submission may be considered only after the exact
private draft, complete provider-visible inventory, all redownloaded bytes, and
a new independent review are frozen.

Automatic approval is treated as a deadline hazard. Before the one submission
attempt, the controller must already hold exact submission authority and exact
approval or cancellation authority, be durably monitored, and bind an absolute
terminal-control deadline at least 12 hours before the documented automatic
approval boundary. No pending state may be left unattended.

The controller submits once, never retries, and continuously reconciles. It
approves immediately only if every check passes; otherwise it cancels before the
bound deadline. Ambiguous responses retain monitoring and escalate without
reusing consumed authority.
